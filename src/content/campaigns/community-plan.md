The planning department has been engaging the Allston-Brighton community in a [community planning process](https://www.bostonplans.org/planning-zoning/planning-initiatives/allston-brighton-community-plan) to determine how the neighborhood should grow and change over the next 20 years. The plan will address housing, transportation, arts, businesses, and other issues that affect the quality of life for residents.

The draft plan is due for release by the end of 2026, and the public will have an opportunity to provide feedback. In the meantime, you can support the changes in the plan by emailing the planning department and letting them know that you **support more housing and transportation improvements** in Allston-Brighton.
