Western Avenue in Lower Allston was the subject of a [years long project](https://www.boston.gov/departments/transportation/project/western-avenue-better-bike-lanes) to improve safety for all road users. While the project was completed in 2025 with the installation of protected bike lanes, the flexposts used to separate the bike lanes from traffic were plowed over by snowplows during the winter. Since then, the city has **refused to replace the flexposts**, and has **refused to engage the public** openly about their future.

Mayor Michelle Wu [promised in July](https://www.wbur.org/news/2026/07/24/boston-mayor-michelle-wu-street-planning-louisa-gag) that Western Ave would be receiving temporary barriers, but as of now, the city has not followed through on that promise. The city must take immediate action to restore protected bike lanes on Western Ave, and ensure that they are maintained year-round.
